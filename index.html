about me

I'm Sara, a Geospatial Analytics PhD student based in Raleigh, NC. My research interests include mitigating human morbidity and mortality related to environmental hazards (e.g., heat, flood, and compound hazards) and pollution, as well as conservation and natural resources management.

I have two ongoing research projects:

  1. How can we utilize data aggregation, cartography, and GIS tools for industry professionals with little to no GIS experience?
  2. How can novel geospatial data and analytical methods reveal environmental uses and benefits that are poorly represented in conventional planning datasets?
    • Unmapped estuarine human uses via spatial planning assessment and inventory.
    • Recording under-observed small-boat movement.
    • Investigating under-identified or inaccessible heat refugia.
